A Missed Death Appointment

Life can not start without a predetermined end. When Noah hears a voice telling him it's his time to die, he dismisses it, shaking off the uneasy feeling of anxiety growing inside. However, Death can not be outrun, even when the God of Death himself struggles to understand the predicament: Noah's heart refuses to stop beating.

Content warnings:

Graphic depiction of violence, suicide themes, death, explicit language. Adviced audience - 16+.
