After his arrival he was quickly whisked away and interrogated. Quickly, the idea of what happened was noted down, with potential to be added as a warning to the manual. 

“So, when I was denied exit, I had to improvise. So, I spent some time walking around, figuring out where another exit could be. My last resort would have been,” Soleum paused, thinking of the best way to get out of the whole ritual thing. He was still uncertain if Braun would share that part with the higher ups in his report. So, the safest bet would be to skip it while still leaving the room to mention it later if needed: “to look for the exit that belonged to another area. In the worst case I would have attempted to run for it in the red zone.”

Good, now he at least could add the ritual incident later if Braun mentions it.

“Good thing I happened to walk right by, Friend.”

The familiar voice sounded from behind him. Kim Soleum turned to be greeted by the amused smile of the Host who non-chalantly walked in on his conversation with the squad. Quickly, the blond man’s eyes went from his face to somewhere above him.

“So, you are under this squad, Mr. Roe Deer…”

For the first time since their meeting something in Braun’s voice gave off a clearly eerie note of aggression. Soleum followed where he was looking, stopping right at the calm face of his Chief Lizard. Oh.

“Mr. Host.” The lizard greeted him.

“Manager Lee.” Braun returned the greeting with a forced polite smile. 

Whatever was between the two made the rest of the conversation die out. 

“Roe,” Supervisor Park carefully tried starting the conversation. “With all that happened we are just glad you are back in one piece. Doesn’t seem like you got contaminated either.”

With that, Kim Soleum hesitated. These people were the closest to any support he could get in the company, and Braun was already in on his secret. 

“Well actually, there is something…”

Carefully, he pulled his jacket sleeve up, revealing the tattoo to his colleagues. 

The three D squad members stared silently at his wrist.

“Yes, Roe?” Deputy Manager Eun asked, raising her eyebrow. “You got a broken bone or?”

Quickly, it clicked. ‘They can’t see it?’

The tattoo was clearly still there. Braun confirmed it too, back at the pa-

Oh.

Could it be that it was only visible at the park? 

He turned towards the Host, still pulling his sleeve up, hoping to get a hint. 

Braun looked at him in confusion. 

“Was our play a little too rough, Friend?” He once again held Soleum’s wrist in his hand, examining it, this time more carefully. “I believe your bones should be quite in perfect condition. It would upset me to know otherwise, Mr. Roe Deer.”

‘He can’t see it?’

Soleum’s mind was racing. He made a mistake. 

“Sorry, I think there is still some phantom pain from when I hit it at the park running away from the bunnies, should be nothing though.”

With a few concerned looks his colleagues dismissed it. 

“Take a rest for the next day, Roe, we will fill in the paperwork and get it all accessed.”

In a way I was thankful. The park was too much to handle, and Kim Soleum would want nothing more than to take a breather. Now, for the last issue to take care of.

The tall blond issue towering above him with an expecting smile.

“At the exit there I was concerned,” Soleum started.

“How come, friend?”

“I never made sure your ticket was complete. I could have put you in a lot of danger, Mr. Host.”

Deciding to remain on good terms with the superior he hoped to never encounter again, he gave Braun a light bow to emphasise his sincerity. 

“My escape would not have been possible without your assistance, thank you!”

Braun stood in silence, waiting for him to finish, then giving out a small chuckle.

“It is boring when things go in the most expected way, don’t you think so, Mr. Roe Deer?”

Soleum felt his frustration creep back in. But also the relief. It was all behind. He whipped sweat off his face, feeling the deer mask still tightly on. In a way it gave him the sudden boost of confidence. The man before him, with the mask off again, in his casual demeanour was studying his every move.
 
It was hard to figure out what to say, but Braun shrugged in a theatrically exaggerated way. 

“I just hope you enjoyed at least some of our little outing, friend,” with that he leaned closer, much closer in, now staring right into the mask’s eyes. Soleum slightly jumped, feeling a hand on his hip, lingering for a moment as if testing something. 

‘What-’

It then went deep into the fabric of his pants pocket, pulling out the familiar plush. The moment Braun straightened, holding the plush in front of himself, studying how it felt in his hands, Soleum felt his heart finally let out a few beats it skipped, his breath he held escaping. This was more stressful than being at the park. The dragon mascots were at least considerate of his personal space. 

“Look, isn’t it a lovely reminder of our little outing?” he shook the toy in front of his face, playfully waving its little paws. 

‘Now what’s on his mind.’

Something about the way the Host spoke gave Soleum a strong feeling that nothing could be predicted about his actions. 

“Will you hold on to it for me, Mr. Roe Deer?” 

Before Kim Soleum could reply, Braun quickly put the toy right into the breast pocket of his now dusty and wrinkled suit.

Soleum refrained from an answer. Instead he nodded. 

“Alright.”

‘As long as it's not cursed to like, watch me when I sleep or something…’

With that, Soleum wished Braun a good evening, and before anything else could be brought up, ran to his dormitory.

In the room, he disregarded checking if his roommate was home, rushing to his bed. The moment he hit the softness of the blanket, his whole body shot in pain of exhaustion. Time to just take his mask off and-

‘Right, the toy…’

He sat up, taking the inanimate bunny from his pocket, and giving it one last judgemental look. This cute little guy was supposed to become his friend, not a random somewhat terrifying unstable colleague of his. With that tired thought, he threw the bunny into the drawer of his bedside table, closing it into the darkness. Taking the mask off, he finally dozed off.
