The three walked through the golden door and saw the setup none of them liked: two chairs, a small table and a leather box. Soleum’s mind raced thinking about what it could mean. 

{Two chairs mean two participants, you can challenge the dealer.}

[But what is the game?]

{The table is too small for a card game, the box is too narrow to hold a standard set of cups, so it’s not dice either.}

Before the two could come to a conclusion, the dealer gestured at the chairs. 

“There are three participants, so I will take the spot for the second game.”

Choi’s hand landed on Soleum’s shoulder the moment he tried to speak. 

“I will face Jaekwan,” he immediately explained himself. “I know going first is worse, but if anything, I don’t trust this to be easy.”

The idea of trying the mysterious game with Kang Yihak was not at all less terrifying, but he nodded. After all, he could understand how important rescuing Ryu Jaekwan was for the agent. They had known each other so long, Soleum could sense the pain in the blue eyes whenever it came to his junior.

Soleum agreed, taking a seat in front of Pony who did not seem too bothered.

“Shall we?” she smiled, thinking about the prize. 

From the corner of his eye Soelum could see the way the agent stared at the box being unlocked, a polished revolver shining brightly in the light of the room. 

“Ok, no, get up!” Choi wanted to drag him off the chair, but both knew it would be a much worse idea. 

Both people in the game stared at the weapon while Ryu Jaekwan loaded in the single bullet. With shaking hands he accepted the gun and cursed under his breath.

{Friend?}

Now that was even worse. His distress seemed to get out of control, worrying Braun. 

[Everything is alright, Braun. Everything is fine.]

He touched the cold barrel, thinking back to the hot coffee in the morning, to the piece of toast with jam and a worried chatter about the weather. He weighted the heavy weapon in his hand thinking of the light touches of Braun’s hands on his when the darkness would think him asleep. 

“Grapes?”

He didn’t realise his body shook until the wet streaks on his cheeks stained the collar of his dusted shirt. 

“Everything is alright, agent. Everything is fine.”

He lifted the gun to his temple and pulled the trigger with a commitment of a doomed man. 

Click.

He threw the gun onto the table and took a shaken breath, pulling the tie loose. What was he just thinking?!

[Braun?]

{Yes, deer?}

He only needed to hear his voice. 

[I will come back for you, ok?]

He had to stay grounded.

{Naturally, Friend.}

Agent Choi dropped to the floor, sitting down when his legs gave up. He could not watch both his rookies like this. 

“Next, please.”

The dealer handed Kang Yihak the weapon and she eagerly put it to her skull. 

“No hard feelings, Roe Deer!” 

She pulled the trigger and cheered when it came through empty. 

“Can I take his spot, I volunteer, I will give the rest of the gold too!” Choi tried to plead with the dealer, but both Jaekwan and Soleum shook their heads. 

“There will be another round, agent.” 

They both knew the weight of those words. No matter who lives now, agent Choi would have to make a decision to stake his life against Jaekwans. 

“Ready?” Kang Yihak nudged him lightly, already feeling the weight of the gold she’d get from the man whose body was to be claimed in full. Down to every unscathed finger.

He picked the gun and pressed it to his temple. 

“Whatever happens next, I trust you will handle the matters well, agent.”

He pulled the trigger.

BANG.



He jumps in the bed and takes a deep breath, feeling like his headache was about to split his head in half. The pain from the bullet cutting right through was still there, slowly subduing little by little. He tried to reach to the spot where it entered, but his hands would not listen, only shaking, gripping into the blanket. 

Soleum looked around, concerned to see the white walls and soft light that was nothing like the casino. 

The last thing he remembered was the weight of the gun, the expression of shock on agent Choi’s face and a very faint voice that must have been Braun’s.

He shivered when the doors to the room opened. 

“You are awake!”

Director Ho placed a tray with tea by his bed and smiled. 

“How did it go?”

There was so much he wanted to say and none of it was acceptable, so he just opened his mouth feeling his throat parched to the state where it hurt. 

“Where is the rest? What happened?” he asked, after taking a moment to drink. 

“You woke up.”

“Explain!”

The director only stood up, listening to the footsteps that followed: fast and with a spring to the step.

“Director, I am back for the compensation!”

Kang Yihak grinned as she waved at Kim Soleum who only stared at his coworker in shock, his mind working overtime. They were alive, unscathed, in the real world.

“Sekwang is a dream, isn’t it?”

“Indeed.”

“Why didn’t you tell us?!”

The director considered his words while writing a note for Pony. 

“The first group that went into sekwang returned after dying to what they described as a forest of hanging ghosts. Everyone except Miss Pony, of course. Did you expect me to make a wild assumption that the return would hold as a rule and send you recklessly exploring with no regard for natural survival?”

It made sense and yet it only made him more angry. Soleum thought back to the way agent Choi stared at him as Soleum pulled the trigger.

He thought of Braun who could not even enter the room, waiting outside, wondering why he could no longer hear him talk back. Wondering what the sound he heard was, before it all went quiet. Dead quiet.

“What happened?” He turned to Kang Yihak and hoped she would at least explain.

“After you left,” she omitted the word “died” which he was grateful for. “The dealer requested a game between me and the agent. Considering I am here, you can make your assumption.”

So, he thought, agent Choi was currently playing a game against Ryu Jaekwan, not knowing that his death would not be permanent.

Even if he does win, what then? He is stuck there, with agent Bronze’s body as a prize. 

He chugged the tea down, unsure how to feel about it. 

“There is nothing we can do but wait now, Mister Roe Deer.” 

Director Ho gave him time to dress and come to the waiting room, where Soleum paced around waiting for news. If they rescue Ryu Jaekwan, where would his body be if Sekwang is a dream? Why was he sent to Sekwang by the wish ticket? The questions were keeping him even more anxious. 

Finally, the doors to the waiting room opened and Director Ho came announcing a new arrival:

“Your friend has awakened.”

Kim Soleum jumped to his feet sprinting to the room where on a clean white bed Braun stretched his arms and stared at Soleum in the doorframe with a quick flash of shock only immediately softened by relief and gratitude. 

{I knew you would not leave me, Friend.}

Soleum felt like his knees were about to give up. He felt drained off life, about to fall, only taking a few more steps to collapse onto the bed where the arms that caught him ruffled his hair. 

{You seem unscathed, I am relieved.}

Once again, he had a lot to say, only opting to silence that could not last long. 

[Wait, how did you wake up?]

It finally dawned on him.

[Braun, what did you do?]

He pushed himself away, studying the face that did not bear any marks, untinted with blood, both ears attached. However, the spark in his right eye was gone, leaving it as dark as a studio without light. 

{When I realised what happened, I made a decision that seemed the most reasonable, Friend.}

Kim Soleum felt himself shake. 

[What did you do…]

{I entrusted the savings to your companions who would need them better, and went to the tracks.}

It was a chilling confession. 

The sting on his hand was like a wake up call. He winced at his own involuntary motion that left the ex-Host shocked. Braun reached to touch where the skin on his face burnt red as it was hit. 

“Sorry.”

He didn’t want to apologise, every part of him was against it, but looking at the way Braun’s eyes betrayed nothing but a surprise, he couldn’t help but feel ashamed. 

The keys in his tattoo burnt again, reminding Soleum that he alone carried the entire reason for the Host’s existence. Without him, Braun was as good as- 

“Take them back.” 

He pushed the hand towards Braun but he only chuckled and gently put a small kiss on the ornament that opened up for him willingly, sending a shiver down Soleum’s spine.

{And who would watch a show hosted by one who can’t hear and can hardly speak?}

[I would.] 

He couldn’t blame him for what he did, not when he was the cause himself. Not when he shot a bullet through his own skull first.

They sit a moment together, as if catching breath after sinking, finally coming safely ashore. Only then, after confirming that Braun could stand up on his own, did the two finally found bravery to walk out. 

“Has anyone else woken up?”

Director Ho shook his head with a smile. It seemed like those who did not know about the waking up, were still caught up in the disaster. Imagining agent Choi carrying the body of his junior was not a comforting picture. 

He relayed the information to Braun who undoubtky understood the idea. 

“Do we wait, or do we go back in?”

He asked more so of himself. Waiting for them to die was a gruesome scenario, and considering agent Choi getting a hold of Ryu Jaekwan’s body it would likely be a long wait - the veteran would not go without a fight. 

“There are shelters in Sekwang,” the director mentioned. “It is possible they are taking cover there.”

Leaving them in the unknown was a worse scenario. 

“We will go back,” Soleum’s hand shook against his own will. He reached to hold Braun’s, reminding himself that he was closer to undoing the damage they’d cause. “We will find them and wake them up.”
