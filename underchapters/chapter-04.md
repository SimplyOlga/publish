Hiding in a vent forever was not an option. Especially after watching a fellow employee get their eyes plucked off, replaced with lenses with a loud pop that sent shivers down Soleum’s spine. 

He knew he still had time, but if anything, he wished to just get reunited with his squad without attracting any other employees to follow him. Even Baek Saheon, his roommate, seemed to be doing alright for now, so there was no moral obligation to spend his time worrying about others. 

He was quite familiar with the way the manor operated, so there was no time to waste: before his own time expired he had to make contact with the D squad as well as preferably discover the way out. However, nothing was going according to plan.

First, he bumped into Go Youngeun who was already nearing her second payment hour, then, a much less pleasant encounter with Baek Saheon followed. Then, with a strike of inspiration (and careful preparation), Soleum guided them through the process of candle crafting, hoping to get them to cover as much of the manor’s ground under covers as possible. And now, with no opportunity to leave, the rookies were catching their breath after watching supervisor Park take off his disguise. It has been one stress after another, leading to Kim Soleum checking his watch knowing quite well - he barely had time left. 

“So, the balcony?” Kim Soleum confirmed.

Park Minseong nodded. 

“Yes, we have been waiting for you there before I decided to look for you myself.”

‘Uhhh…’

As heartwarming as it was, this put the trio at a much less advantageous spot. Only three people could fit under the candlelight. Out of the four, Kim Soleum had the highest bet at surviving without… 

With a heavy heart beating insanely fast, a decision was made
. 
Sending the two other rookies and his supervisor away under the light of the candle, Soleum ran through the hallway to the next staircase. 

“I will go downstairs, you will go up. In a couple floors we are to trace back our steps and assemble at the balcony. If you find the exit floor, do not wait for me. Pick up Manager Lee and Deputy manager Eun, leave a note at the balcony on where the exit is and leave. I will do the same if I am there before you.”

With no time to lose, splitting into two groups was the best option: covering the ground and sharing the intel later would give them a higher chance at survival. 

 ‘Ughhh…’ 

Still, as he ran along among still semi conscious bodies on the floor, the thought was not helping much.

After a deep descent, he had 5 floors to go through. Now, at B5, he stared at the mechanical spider, swallowing his fear. 

“When the guests end their visit, do they go up the floor?”

It all depended on this. If it was a no, he had no chance predicting just how deep they would have to venture to get out. 

With a metallic scratch, the monstrous head nodded. 

Soleum’s stomach did a flip. With zero hesitation, he rushed back to the stairs. He barely had 15 minutes to finish his check…

And 15 minutes later, bent behind a couch at the B2 lobby, he cursed. This was less than ideal. Still, it left him with just two options, so all he had to do was regroup with the squad or get their memo about the location of the exit. In any case staying here alone was a suicide. 
Holding his breath, he listened to the metallic monster pass by, disappearing behind a corner to another exhibition segment. Carefully, he rose up, one slow but quiet and precise step after another, reaching closer and closer towards the way out before tripping over with a loud thud. Solem felt his head echo in sharp pain. As he shook it off, he felt a tight grab on his leg. A person missing eyes, mouth and almost all limbs, was desperately grabbing onto his leg, convulsing in pain. Shaking off the arm, his heart sunk: the mechanical steps were getting closer and closer, approaching, undeniably set on catching him. 

With all his might, Soleum sprung to his feet, rushing out of the room, hearing the scratch of metal feet on the floor behind dragging at a noticeably higher speed. 

‘Uhhhhh!’

Suddenly the decision to give out his candle nagged at him. If only he could find someone else, someone with preferably more time before the next fee collection. Another turn, his loud steps now echoing in the maze of the exhibition, the eyes on the grotesque painting following his escape with teary expression. Another turn. 

‘Anyone, just anyon-’

And there, at the end of the corridor leading to the emergency staircase door, a figure was crouching over something on the ground. A human figure. 

As his steps grew faster, the figure rose up, ready to run away from the approaching metal claws. 

“Do you have time before yo-” Soleum screamed, before stopping in his tracks. 

Just behind the figure, another metal beast rose from the floor. As if limping, it trembled attempting to keep its balance. The wide mouth with spiked teeth opened up, prepared to engulf the employee right before Soleum’s eyes. In the darkness of the poorly lit passage, the metal sparkled with a chilling cold light.

“Watch out!”

Without even thinking, Kim Soleum was ready to risk it, push the person away, hoping the sacrifice of his eyes or ears would not be in vain. 

Then, in barely a second, the passage lit up, blindingly bright. The wave of heat burnt Soleum’s masked face, causing him to stop, covering his head. As if a scorching heat of an oven was suddenly blasted open onto him.

A loud shriek went through the room, ringing in his ears in a blood chilling screech. Soleum managed to open his eyes. 

Before him, half of the corridor lit up in flames. The machinery crumbled to the floor, the blood coloured oil spilling from its broken jaw, catching up in flame, sending grotesque shadows dancing on the walls. Among them, one stood eerily still, untouched by the light, static. 

The long shadow cast by the figure of who he presumed to be human. The figure stood motionless, hands in pockets of what was now lit up by the dancing lights to be a long coat. The coat that seemed to be so familiar…

With a loud scratch, a claw dug into the flooring behind him, a massive mechanical beast emerging behind him. Caught up, jaw wide open, its arms reached out to grab his body, making Soleum instinctively jump backwards. 

Choosing between the clawed machine and the eerie presence behind, between the loss of his eyes and the unknown… With determination driven by fear, Soelum knelt slightly: there was still a chance he could jump over the hands, get himself out of this tight spot and avoid the pursuit of either of them. With a sharp inhale, he prepared to run. 

The metallic beast in front of him set up aflame. Without sound. Without a spark. With no oily blood or wound. It shrieked and sank lower and lower, the metal melting in seconds, dripping on the floor, uncovering the horrific structure of the inner skeleton of the exhibition guard.

With the heat burning at his skin and his heart pounding in his chest, he turned around. Whatever was in here with him was not something from the Dark Exploration records. There was nothing ever mentioned in the wiki about flames raging in the manor. Meaning… from here on he was on his own, in uncharted territory. Deep underground of this darkness. Stuck with a being capable of incinerating him on the spot within a second. And yet, he was still standing there, in the silence that followed as the metal beast collapsed. 

Quietly, the figure moved. Turning its head slowly to look at him, dying out lights still dancing on the silhouette. 

The pick black screen stood watching him. Just like in his nightmares from before. 

This couldn’t be. 

The black coat, the suit, the gloves. And now the TV screen. A TV screen clearly a part of a full TV box, sitting on the man's shoulders instead of a head. 

“Braun” fully turned around. Tilting his “head” slightly to the side, a surprised emote popped up on the screen.

[My Deer friend!]

The voice, sounding like it was rendered through old speakers, made Soleum swallow hard. 

Even the voice matched. 

‘No, this is wrong…’

The thoughts running through his head replaced one another, hectically trying to make sense of this. There were no records of this in the wiki. This had to be an external darkness. One that potentially imitates someone you know, or maybe thrives off your dreams or fears. No, there was no reason for the darkness to attack the machines purposefully keeping him unharmed. One thought that was left was…

‘Contamination...’

With the background Braun had there was no way he could avoid contamination. Considering his manner of walking around without a mask, his high grade exploration and solo work… 

Soleum had yet to witness contamination in person and now was the worst time. Someone as strong as Braun, with Soleum left all alone, deep underground where no one would return to look for him, even his squad. 


The figure took a step closer, tired of waiting for a reply.

[You are brave to be venturing so deep alone,] he murmured, a bit of static noise mixing in with the sound of his voice. 


Still, contamination would normally take on a form related to the darkness it reached the limit in. So why was Braun affected in such a peculiar way…

With the man walking closer and Soleum backing as much as he could, his escape blocked by the scorching hot remains of the metal body behind, he could take a proper look at the “mask”.

It was a solid old TV model, very similar to the one Braun’s mask was imitating. It sat on his now pitch black neck, swaying lightly with his every step. As if the mask became one with the Host…

As Braun stopped just a step away from fully cornering Kim Soleum, the emote on his face switched to a thinking one. 

[Hm. I suppose this appearance would leave a person speechless,” he murmured as if thinking out loud. The emote switched back to an excited one. “Do not worry, friend, there is no one left to disturb us.]

‘Disturb us…’

So, Soleum thought, he was still conscious enough to not kill him on the spot. 

“Thank you…” was all he could muster the courage to say. 

The antennas on the Host’s head waved a little in satisfaction. 

[Ah, I’ve missed your voice! For a moment there I was not certain I hadn’t mistaken you for someone else! How rude would that have been…]

Braun theatrically put his hand on his chest, as if imitating deep pain of offense. 

[What good host would not remember his dear guests!]

‘Host? Could it be…’

WIth a bit of courage, his throat dry and aching, Soleum continued, probing:

“Thank you, for your care and hospitality…”

Braun’s face lit up with a pleased emote. 

Was it possible, Kim Soleum wondered, that the contamination took on a form of his own mask? Merging his body and soul with the mask born out of his identity as a Host, since they were already connected enough to cause pain upon forced separation. Soleum recalled the feeling he had when Braun tried to take his mask off…

But that power of incineration was just too much. Now this entity leaned towards him, towering over. 

[Your company is gratitude enough, my Friend. But I can imagine you wish to leave this place now that your presence has attracted attention I would rather not share with anyone as rude as them.]

He lightly kicked at the metallic body at his feet, the fabric of his clothes ignoring the flames which Soleum could still vividly feel the heat of it.

‘This is insanity. But if I follow along and don’t anger him, perhaps I might just make it out…’

With every guard on the floor alert of his presence, likely hunting Baun down as well for the destruction of… property. 

“Yes, I would love to leave, Mr. Host,” he started, carefully choosing his words. “But of course, cutting our time together so short would be a complete shame. After all, our show just started.”

Braun paused, studying him. 

‘Did I fuck up?’




‘A show?’ 

Braun paused. 

‘Oh.’

‘Oh this is delightful.’

Something inside of him sparked at hearing familiar words, directed at him, from a wonderful guest to an esteemed Host. 

‘Does he think I am-’

Braun could feel the darkness in him, like an overflowing current, spinning his head in an ever so pleasant light dizziness. A state of euphoria he’d long missed since the day he lost the studio…

Perhaps, this was an even more delightful turn of events. His wonderful deer friend had no idea exactly what he was dealing with. And yet, eager to chase that nostalgic feeling, he took a deep breath, feeling the essence of the Manor ignite a new kind of desire in him.

‘This is a dance I could not have asked for a better partner for.’

[Indeed, how can a show begin without the introduction of the guests!]


As if by an unspoken command, a pitch black microphone materialised in the hand of the host, the wire going from the connecting port to somewhere on the back of his head. 

Braun pushed it into Soleum’s hands, encouraging him to speak.
 
The dark fabric was cold to the touch, matte and sticking to his skin ever so slightly. It felt like now he would not be able to get it off his skin.

[I am sure our audience would love to know more about you, Mr. Roe Deer!]

Kim Soleum jolted at the wave of heat that burnt into his back, turning around only now noticing one of the guards catching flame at the end of the corridor. 

“I am not sure,” he started, walking beside Braun only as far as the cable allowed. “What would the audience like to know?”


At this point every guard around would be alert about their presence, disregarding anyone and anything on the way. Of course with this “Braun” by his side, if things went on smoothly, there was little to be afraid of. 

There was a lot to be afraid of.

[Well, for example, what does our Friend love to do in his spare time?]


Soleum felt like this whole absurd situation could not play out any more stressful. He gently pulled Braun by the wire, leading him to the staircase door. If he wanted to make it out alive, he had to put on his best performance.

With a wide smile, looking right at the Host and opening the door for him with an inviting gesture, he laughed. 

“Nothing beats a nice TV show before bed, am I right, Mr. Host?”

Braun’s antennas shot up in delight, his screen going up in a wave of static. 
Together, they slipped onto the staircase, leaving the half destroyed hallway behind. 
Two floors left to confirm still.

[My oh my, I wonder what shows you prefer, my friend!]

His speaker-like voice echoed in the small space. Only now did Soleum realise, climbing the stairs ahead of Braun-

Two, three steps ahead, his voice was still coming from above. Soleum turned around.

‘Just how tall… is he’

This only confirmed: his colleague was no longer human. The only hope Kim Soleum held was that upon reaching the exit he could return the favour from the amusement park and push him out. If there was hope to be had for him, isolating him from the darkness right into the company could maybe get the security team involved. 

For now what they had to prioritise was getting the answer from the last two floors. The higher they went the higher their survival chances were getting. 

“Well,” he continued, “I like shows with memorable characters that get to work together to overcome obstacles on their way.”

Soleum’s eyes were darting as they stepped onto the next floor. Now, a new problem presented itself. He had no way of asking the guards questions without first sacrificing a part of himself. And he was quite fond of all of his parts. 

Was it possible that Braun could qualify as a visitor without a fee due?
 
Before he could even ask, a familiar scent of cologne enveloped him, the dark fabric of the heavy black coat falling over his shoulder. 

‘That’s right!’ 

The same way they hid their presence in the amusement park, now Braun’s coat was concealing them from the view of the manor guards. While that could give them a chance to escape to the upper floors and meet up with the others, there was still a threat of uncertainty of their success. And he was not ready to risk it all by bringing the contaminated Host over. No, he had to make sure about the exit. After all, the candle could only give the other so much time…

Suddenly, the weight of the coat on his shoulders shifted. Suddenly he sunk in the fabric, his knees slightly bending over the doubled weight of it. Adjusting it, he looked at the Host, who was now, for the first time since their first meeting, in just his suit. His body felt uncanny large, sending Soleum’s instincts into panic.

‘Was he even a human still?’

[I see your point, Mr. Roe Deer.]

Braun casually walked ahead of him, right into the depths of the exhibition hall. With Soleum hidden from the perception of the darkness, he had to admit there was curiosity rising in him. How would the local darkness react to someone clearly contaminated and yet not from this setting. Someone who just set half a floor on fire. 

In just a moment Braun spotted a guard. Carefully taking the microphone from Soleum’s hands, he winked with a little face on his screen and approached the machine. 


Everything in Soleum’s body screamed: RUN. 

This was his chance. Braun was distracted, his hand was no longer bound by the microphone cable, and even the coat on his shoulders would help to avoid pursuit until he would shake the Host off. After all, it was working on the local darkness. 

‘Then how did he just…’

A few conflicting thoughts went through his head. 

In the park Braun admitted to having issues seeing him with the coat on. Here, he just walked up to him looking right at his masked face. 

So, what was it… 


[My dear friend, what was it that you would like me to ask?]


Snapping back from his thoughts, Kim Soleum turned to check on Braun and was lost for words.

The machine guard, clearly aware of his presence, stared right at the Host. With barely two meters between them it almost looked like the guard… was afraid to move. Afraid to take the first action. 

“Could you please ask the fellow guest if the local visitors, upon finishing their tour, go up the floor?”

Soleum could swear, the sound of his voice triggered the machine. It was ready to turn towards him and yet with pained restraint it stood still, looking at the Host who brought his microphone closer to the metallic face. 

[You heard my dear guest. Would you like to share with us some of the intricacies of your work?]

The guard nodded its head, fighting the urge to run straight for Kim Soleum. 

So, they really only had one floor left. The exit must be on floor B1. Kim Soleum exhaled in relief. 

[Hm, I have to admit you are not the most inviting guest to interview.]

Something in the tone of Braun’s voice made Soleum hide further into the coat, still hoping it would protect him.

How he wished for the candle he left with the others. 

[A good guest knows that short uninspired answers are as much of an insult to a welcoming host as a harsh review in the papers the morning after the show! Wouldn’t you agree Mr. Roe Deer?]

Before Soleum could answer, the whole of the guard's body lit up aflame in front of him, once again melting in seconds. He stepped back, dodging the spilling oil and metal, feeling the air burn his masked face. 

‘One wrong move, one wrong line and I will end up a pile of ash at best.’

The thought made him swallow, sweat beading on his neck. 

“I suppose we might want to look for better guests for your show, Mr. Host.”

Braun turned around and delightfully shot his antennae up seeing Kim Soleum eagerly stretching his arm to pick back the microphone. 

Together, they walked up one more flight of stairs. One last guard stood before them and the exit. But how was he supposed to ask Braun for assistance with this. 

[Like I said before, you are like a breath of fresh air, my friend! So go on, please do tell me more about yourself.]

He had to quickly come up with something, anything exciting enough to not anger the Host.

[Do you perhaps have any little secrets you might wish to share with your loyal fan?]

Soleum’s heart skipped a beat. That’s it, this was his way out. 

He opened the door to B1 in front of the Host, but instead of walking ahead, Braun held the door himself, giant arm stretched above Soleum’s head, once again reminding him: he could snap his neck in just an effortless move. 

[After you, friend.]

Soleum stepped into the new exhibition hall with unease. He had to take this gamble. 

“Well, I do actually have a few secrets I have not told anyone before, Mr. Host.”

Braun’s face lit up in excitement. 

“And I think it would make for a perfect opportunity to share it, here, with you, on this show!”

Soleum forced himself to playfully wink at the Host. 

[Well well, count me excited!]

Soleum, however, started walking forward, still hidden by the coat, pulling Braun after himself with the cord.

‘I have a darkness on a leash…’

What an absurd thought. 

“But secrets are something that isn’t easy to let go of, of course.”

[I understand, a good host should always take the time to build up the trust and reputation to let his guest feel welcome and safe to open up!]

Soleum pretended to ponder it. 

“Oh please, Mr. Host, I already trust you more than anyone! After all,” he playfully tugged at the cord leading to the TV head. “I feel like no one understands me quite as well as you do.”

Everything in his body wanted to run. The proximity to the contaminated elite, the absurdity of it all.

“So, what I mean is that every secret comes at a little price, to mark the significance of it.”

Braun leaned in closer, cutting the distance between them. Just now, Soleum could easily guess his height must have passed 3 meters for certain. 

[Just name your price, my Friend. This Braun will see to it. But in return, you will indulge me with something no one has ever known about you.]

The Host’s hand picked Kim Soleum’s chin and lifted it up, as if inspecting his face. 

‘Will he try to rip my mask off again?’

Just the thought of it was mortifying. In this form, it would take Braun no effort to shred his mask and skin along with it, ignoring any plea or scream of agony from him…

“Don’t even doubt it, it will be something I have kept to myself only.”

Braun nodded in satisfaction, picking the microphone and walking to the nearest guard. 

[When the guests of your estate take their leave, do they go up the floor?]

The machine nodded. 

‘This can not be…’

“No, no, this is wrong,” Kim Soleum exhaled in frustration. 

The machine shifted its gaze on him. 

‘Fuck.’

How careless of him. 

The claws flew at him and just as they were about to rip into his flesh, the machine lit up in flames. 

Watching it melt onto the ground sent a sick feeling of relief and anger. Why did it even move? 

Regardless, at a more pressing matter - they were screwed. 

[You seem displeased, Friend.]

Soleum did not respond. 

What was it that he missed? 

“Mr. Host,” he probed. “Someone as talented and experienced at interviewing guests surely knows how to tell if someone is lying, right?”

[Naturally.]

“Was this rude guest you just… disposed of - lying?”

[Not at all, Friend.]

‘Fuck.’

“Mister Host, I can not wait to introduce you to some marvelous friends of mine!”

That’s right. His only hope now was to at least reunite with the others. Surely Section Chief would be of assistance…

[But why? I thought you wished to leave this place first.]

Soleum looked at Braun with an uneasy feeling. Was there something he missed? 

[I see your confusion, Friend. But this Braun has a little secret of his own he is willing to trade. A secret that might just help you find what you are looking for.]

“Do you know where it is? The exit?”

[Perhaps. A good Host always makes sure to research his venue way ahead of time :)]

‘Did he know THIS WHOLE TIME?’

Soleum could feel anger rising in him, but this was not the time.

[And just like you told me, every little secret is worth a trade. If I tell you, you will owe me one secret and one favour, to make it a fair trade for my services!]

Kim Soleum swallowed. 

‘Will he ask me to take off my mask?’

Whatever it would be, he can deal with it with enough stalling.

“Of course.”

[Just follow me, Friend. It is up to a good Host to take care and escort the guests out when it is time to end the show.]


Pushing him in the back, the Host led him back to the stairs. As the went up, instead of going all the way to the first floor, the Host paused and explained. 

[It is good to be attentive to differences in cultures of my guests. So, in time I learnt a few things about the most peculiar of differences between all of my guests. For example, some of my guests would get lost trying to run around the studio expecting to find the B0 floor.]

“Of course!”

The relief Kim Soleum felt as he saw the door in the middle of the flight of stairs made him give Braun a genuine wide smile. 

“There is another way to name the first floor!”

Braun’s screen flashed a little bell emote, accompanied by a ringing sound.

[Ring Ring! Correct!]

Soleum rushed to the giant glass door, barely holding his excitement after everything he was put through. 

But there was no way he could leave yet.

“Mr. Host, do you remember I told you I have some fascinating friends I would love for you to meet?”

He pointed shyly to the microphone the Host was still carrying. 

“Do you think we could, maybe, call for them?”

[Oh, you want to use your “Call a Friend” shot? Why of course!]

Taking the dark cold microphone willingly made Soleum question if it was him who was contaminated here. 

[[[ATTENTION ATTENTION! THIS ANNOUNCEMENT WILL ONLY PLAY ONCE! THE EXIT FROM THE EXHIBITION IS LOCATED ON FLOOR ZERO ACCESSIBLE THROUGH A DOOR BETWEEN F1 AND B1!]]]

His lungs fell massive, his voice thunderingly loud. There was no way anyone could miss it. 
With clear conscience he was now confident D squad and the others would at least have a solid chance to make it out. 

Now, for the pressing matters. To repay Braun for the push and shove at the park. 

Still holding onto the microphone, he led the Host to the gates. The microphone still almost glued to his hands, the cord tightly leading the darkness behind him. 

“As I said, there is a secret I have not told anyone.”

Braun eagerly followed:

[Believe me, Friend, you can entrust it to me!]

Kim Soleum felt his back bumping into the cold stained glass. With one arm he found the door handle behind him, with the other he started pulling the microphone cable in loops over his wrist, forcing the Host to get closer and closer, until he was almost touching the screen with the horns of his deer mask. 

“It is related to our abrupt parting back at the park…”

[ ! ]

“In truth,” Kim Soleum clicked the lock on the door. “I shoved that toy you gave me into the furthest, dustiest nook of my room!”

With a swift motion he kicked the door open and prayed his strength was enough. In a harsh movement he pulled at the cable feeling both of them falling right through the exit door.
