After everything he went through, at long last luck was on his side. Holding his suitcase in anticipation, Kim Soleum stepped through the doors to the mall that promised him not only a peaceful day on the job, but also a prospect of loot. 

Parting ways with his colleagues, he confidently stepped down into the lower floors of the darkness. Even knowing how it worked and how there was no danger in being here, it still brought an uneasy feeling onto him. After all, last time we went downstairs in a darkness was a mess to put it simply. A disaster to put it closer to the real scope of things. 

But now his luck was finally starting to turn. 

Quickly weighing his briefcase, he placed it onto a nearby pavement, clicking the lock open to double check the contents: a big expensive box of pearls, a set of stickers, his dream essence collector along with the company stuff and… the plush. Soleum picked the keychain into his hand giving it a firm squish. A very first squish. Admittedly, the plush was cute. The little face looked friendly. However, Soleum still had not got rid of seeing the murderous intent of the mascot behind the little smile. Along with a phantom feeling of strong hands pinning him and squeezing his ribcage. Not a good association, he thought, returning the plush to the briefcase. After all, now he at least held on to the “payback” promise.

Soleum added some speed to his walk. It was sheer anticipation of what he could only call the best ghost story one can stumble upon. 

Finally, after carefully walking in the needed pattern, the store appeared just as described in the records. 

The moonlight tattoo shop.

Having greeted the owner and presented her with the shimmering set of blue pearls, Kim Soleum beamed with joy and anticipation. If there was one way to make his life in this universe easier it was through this very shop.

Having received a booklet and a drink, he spent quite a bit of time flipping through the pages, uncertain of his decision. Overall it was hard to make a choice whenever it was as important as this, but now he wished he had someone to ask for assistance. A bell rang at the door, startling Kim Soleum, but the master gestured to him to just walk to the back of the parlour and take his time making the decision before disappearing to tend to the client.

‘It’s unlikely someone from the squad made it here,’ Kim Soleum thought.

But after all, the shops here were meant to imitate actual living environments so he decided to treat the situation as nothing but world-building ambiance. Focusing again on the designs, he tried to imagine just which one of these could possibly hint to something related to courage. Out of desperation, Soleum leaned at the chair and tapped on the surface of his briefcase. A weird impulsive thought struck him, making him pull the plush out of the case.

“Now imagine if the ritual back then worked, how useful would it be to have a darkness now to give me a hint?”

He spoke to the toy placed on the side table. As expected, the toy did not reciprocate.
 
“Courage, bravery, fearless acts, what could it possibly be…”

He flipped through the pages finally set to ask the master as soon as she was back.
As soon as she walked back with a new drink and an apology for the distraction, he handed the book back with the question. 

“Could you please help me pick something to do with courage?”

The master hesitantly nodded before opening a page with a design resembling a tomato plant. Intricate shape, beautiful lines, but still an unexpected choice. Still, Soleum had to trust the process. 

“I will take this one please.”

The anticipation within him was reaching a peak. This will make his life so much easier!

The master shook her head apologetically. 

[This design has already been reserved.]

‘What.’

But if it was in the book it had to be available.

“You mean you can’t do it for me because someone has already paid for this one specifically?”

The master nodded. 

“Can you not do the same tattoo for two different individuals?”

He already knew the answer and yet his heart sank when the master shook her head. 

‘No, no, come on!’

This was the closest he ever got to making his life in this mad universe somewhat bearable. And it was snatched from his arms right in front of him. But this made no sense, the design was in the booklet so whoever snatched it had to had done it ri-

The door to the room slowly opened, letting the cozy light of the workspace leak onto the darkness of the lobby. And then a figure appeared, an awfully familiar figure. 

“Oh my, this is quite not a place I was expecting to bump into you, Mr. Roe Deer.”


The familiar coat, the suit, and the absence of mask revealing a bright smile. The last part made him feel relieved. If anything, seeing his face instead of the TV head was comforting. 
The contamination tests for the employees involved in the manor incident came negative but Kim Soleum perfectly knew that nothing would stop this company from adjusting results, releasing a contaminated employee with no investigation, or even abusing the situation to their advantage. After all, the Security team was running and operational.

“Nice to see you well, Mr. Host,” Kim Soleum said carefully. After all, he was uncertain just where they were standing and how much of their last meeting would resurface now. The TV head, the flames, their parting. 

Braun walked into the workspace confidently, scanning the room before his eyes fell on the small plush on the table. Kim Soleum cursed himself mentally for not hiding it away. But at the end of the day, wasn’t this what he wanted? Him carrying the thing around?

“Ah, how sweet it is to see a familiar face,” he absolutely beamed with joy. 

But the moment the attention was shifted to the toy, the tattoo master gasped and rushed to grab it before Soleum could stop her or say anything. Quickly she nodded her head. 

[Part of the special services.]

And excitedly she rushed out of the room with the cotton bunny in her hands. 

As the door closed leaving the two men behind, Kim Soleum felt the unease of the moment. In this darkness, alone, Braun could do whatever he wished to him with no witnesses. How little would be needed to set the whole place on fire, having his squad members report another victim fallen to the “unpredictable dangers of the mall”.

“Happy to see you stick to your end of the bargain, Mr. Roe Deer,” Braun walked up closer.

Just now Kim Soleum realised that him sitting comfortably on the laidback chair was a naive move that now left him no escape. He awkwardly watched the man walk up and sit on the side of his chair. A heavy silence fell in the room. 

Braun took the booklet from his hands and leisurely flipped through a few pages. 

“Didn’t think you’d be one to venture to such a distant forgotten place, Mr. Roe Deer. How did you find your way here?” 

There was a mischievous glimmer in the Host’s eyes that made Soleum feel unnerved. 

‘Does he suspect me…’

The ritual, his actions in the Manor, now this…

“The rest of my squad went to the upper floors so I was sent down here,” he replied, imitating a tremble of fear in his voice. “This place looked like something I could manage on my own.”

Considering how common harassment based on hierarchy was in the elite squads, there was a chance Braun would believe this. Of course, he didn’t want to present D squad in such a light but who would care.

“Found anything to your liking?” Braun resumed his absentminded investigation of the booklet. 

“No, not really.”

Kim Soleum was unsure just how much he could say. On one hand, Braun has already seen quite a bit of rather suspicious actions from him. If he was to report him, would something like getting an “accidental darkness tattoo” really make a difference. But on the other hand, just how much was he willing to share with the man who is clearly contaminated with no saying how well his control holds. 

Suddenly, a thought occurred to him. 

“I was just thinking I could cover something up…” 

With that, he grabbed onto the left sleeve of his jacket, noticing Braun’s eyes catch onto the movement. Ever since the return from the amusement park the tattoo bothered him. Worse than that - he now had another. Neither of them were visible to anyone but himself, but the way Braun read the word “socius” back at the park never slipped his attention. 

“Ah, the other little memento from our picnic?”

‘So he remembers it!’

Frustration was rising in Soleum. This man was unpredictable, uncontrollable and unreliable. Except, maybe now he could be of some assistance.

Kim Soleum pulled his sleeve a bit up, revealing the two tattoos. 

“Oh, how come you got this?”

Braun set the book aside and carefully took his wrist into his large gloved hand. Kim Soleum jolted at the touch, but didn’t oppose. Better be compliant than incinerated. 

“It was in a random darkness off duty. But in the end the local ghost let me sign a contract to come into ownership on an estate.”

Braun rubbed circles at the symbols. 

“You should be more careful, Mr. Roe Deer. Accepting contracts with darkness like this left and right is quite risky.”

Kim Soleum pulled his arm away.

“I appreciate your concern. I will be fine.”

Braun laughed. 

“That I don’t doubt. After all it was you who wanted to ‘befriend a darkness’ not me.”

Soleum wanted to drop dead right there and then. Somehow the way it was brought up made him feel like an embarrassing memory was resurfacing. 



The doors to the workspace opened again and the master walked in with a joyous smile, handing the little plush back into Soleum’s arms. Somehow, it looked newer: washed, refreshed and smelling of a nice soap. 

“Oh, now you really ought to keep good care of our friend. It would be a shame to see him go back to the ragged state.”

Braun quickly slid off the chair and relocated to a couch in the corner of the room, picking up one of the drinks left there for Soleum. His spot was taken by the master in a second, her expectedly looking at Soleum, waiting for his choice. 

“Well, I know you said the design I wanted was taken but maybe you could figure something out to cover this?”

At the first sight of the symbols, her eyes widened, then she hesitantly nodded. And so the room fell into silence only filled with a quiet buzz of the moonlit machine. Kim Soleum relaxed a little with the plush in his hands, master at work, and Braun in the corner quietly reading some magazine over a cup of Soleum’s coffee.

‘What is he even doing here?’ 

But even with an unexpecting company, there was a sense of peace and anticipation over this once in a life opportunity. 

The moment the machine’s buzz was over and the master lightly tapped his shoulder, Soleum almost felt like he was brought back from a small nap. Over him, the master was proudly anticipating his verdict. 

“Wow, it’s gorgeous,” was all he could say looking at the fresh ink shaping into the starry moonlit scenery on his wrist. He knew it would vanish the next day, but he could still appreciate the craftsmanship. 

“What a fine work indeed,” Braun said, standing right behind him and inspecting it over Soleum’s shoulder. 

The master, however, kept gesturing to the tattoo, then holding her fingers to her own wrist. 
“I think I get the idea,” Braun chuckled and then took the toy out from Soleum’s hand. “Don’t get startled, Friend.”

‘If anything, this makes me only more concerned,’ Soleum thought but didn’t move. 
Braun held the plush close to the tattoo, making him instinctively shiver expecting pain. Instead an odd sensation rippled through him when the gloved hands pressed the toy into his skin: deeper and deeper. 

Astonished, Soleum watched the whole thing just disappear into his wrist. There was no pain, just an odd sense of… disturbance. He didn’t feel it “inside of him” but something instinctively made him aware of the intrusion. Especially when Braun didn’t stop.

Kim Soleum jolted, feeling the gloved warm fingers follow the toy inside, merging in with his skin, crawling under the ink of the tattoo, distorting the design. He could almost see the outline of his fingers under his skin. As an outside observer it would have definitely made Soleum uneasy, but now he was admiring it speechlessly. Something about the way the entrance to his tattoo stretched and itched at the intrusion, the way his whole body was subconsciously aware of every millimetre of the fingers inside of him, feeling every single curve and touch intently…

He pulled his arm away, feeling something akin to cold inside the tattoo at the loss of contact. 

“Fascinating, you have a whole another dimension connected to your tattoo now,” Braun added as Soleum reached inside it himself pulling the toy out. 

‘I will need to experiment with this later…’

The toy seemed intact and still smelling of soap. 

“Thank you so much, it’s amazing,” Soleum said to the happy master who was delighted to see her work appreciated. 

[I still owe you a design for the price you paid.]

‘Oh, does she mean as a compensation for the tomato one not being available?’

Soleum reached for the catalogue but a gloved hand was faster. Braun flipped through the pages and pointed at one of the designs. 

“How about this one?”

Kim Soleum nervously swallowed. Of course there was nothing among the designs that was harmful, useless at worst, but still the idea of Braun picking for him was nerve wracking. The master took the booklet and left the room after seeing a hesitant nod of agreement from Kim Soleum.

“Why don’t you get the design you like for yourself? My treat as a payback for… our last escape.”

There was no better way to describe the manor incident without bringing anything unpleasant back. 

Braun smiled eye to eye. 

“Oh, thank you, friend, but I have already purchased one today.”

‘Wait.’

‘Don’t tell me…’

The master returned, holding a set of small papers out to him. Temporary tattoos, he recognised. Flipping them around Kim Soleum was greeted with an unexpected and yet somewhat predictable design. 

A camera film spilling out, tied into a bow at the end. 

‘I just hope this doesn’t have some weird ability…’

Politely, he thanked the master and stood up, picking his briefcase and temporarily putting the soft clean plush into his chest pocket. Braun followed him outside. 


“The night is young, friend, would you like to join me on an errand?”

Kim Soleum wanted nothing less. 

“Alright.”


It was hard to explain why he agreed. Something in the Braun talked was always soothing, catching you comfortably off guard but not out of ill intent, more so to engage in a more intimate way.


“Do you see yourself as a curious person, Mr. Roe Deer?” Braun asked as they walked to an elevator, waiting for it to descend to their level. 

“A little, I guess. In a way doesn’t everyone like something new from time to time?”

‘Like getting a darkness tattoo recommended by a contaminated lunatic.’

Braun hummed as if contemplating. 

“Then aren’t you curious about me?”

The elevator let out a small ring of a bell before opening the doors. The cold light of the cabin streamed onto the concrete of the parking lots. Braun walked in and turned around: leaning onto a wall he was waiting for Soleum to join. Direct eye contact, as if to test him, not missing a single emotion that could give something away.




His little deer stood there lit up by the cold blue light hesitant to follow. It took him a moment to take a breath he thought Braun would not notice to step inside the narrow elevator cabin.

“I try to restrain my curiosity when it comes to my coworkers,” he finally replied as the elevator started its delightfully slow ascend.  

“How come?”

In the small space Braun could hear his breath, feel the way the antlers of his mask would sometimes brush over his jacket. His mind went back to the peculiar tattoo on his wrist. 

The warmth of his insides as Braun’s fingers went deeper, the shiver of his flesh felt from within. Just the thought of it sent a warm feeling through his bones. How he wished at this very moment to slam his fist into the elevator controls, to pull out the cables and tie them around his friend’s wrists, to demand his full attention on him alone. The new peculiar quality of the tattoo was the only thing stopping him from ripping the skin with the mark off his wrist in a warning to never let a darkness claim him so easily. How naive does his Roe Deer have to be to act so impulsively with his own body. To give a part of it to someone. To let them mark him. All the while keeping himself so distant with him. 

“I know every person in the company has a reason to be there, a reason to go through what we go through with a goal in mind. If they ever desire to share their reasons with me - I will listen. Otherwise, I believe there is a mutual understanding over the privacy of our motives.”

[How secretive,] Braun thought.

He wondered, would there ever be a day Roe Deer would look into his eyes without the mask, telling him every single worry and concern, his every wish and fear, his every memory and goal. The fundamental essence of a human: their did’s and will’s, their do’s and dont’s. Braun would love to hear them again and again, memorise them. Write them down from memory hundreds of times if it could ever bring him closer to understanding.

“Do you think there are people among them who just do it for the sake of it all?”

The Deer mask turned to him. 

“Absolutely.”

Braun loved the answer. It felt like both judgement and acceptance: it was something to be frowned upon and yet something admittedly too human-like in nature to avoid. 




The elevator bell rang again and with a soft push the cabin came to a stop. The doors opened to the warm and cozy light of a luxury boutique floor of the mall.

‘Of course he’d head for here,’ Soleum thought.
