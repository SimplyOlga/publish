Passing through the realities always sends an odd tingle through your body. Entering a darkness could be described in a predictable turn of phrase: you feel a shiver run down your spine. Returning to the human realm was, however, more complicated to describe. It was, as simple as you can put, similar to a huge weight suddenly falling onto your shoulders. As if returning back to your senses, back to the very essence of existence: a massive sense of conscious presence would push onto you. It was a feeling that could not be mistaken for anything else. Just like a child when born starts crying - returning to the real world would almost make you want to curl in and shiver in pity. 

But that was purely a physical manifestation. An average returnee would always disregard such feelings: the joy of return leaving no space for such gloomy throughs. It is a fundamental, deeply rooted nostalgia for you home. Even if the darkness promises you freedom, joy, liberation from mortal struggles - we push it aside in favour of something familiar and less alien. 

Now, if you take the joy of returning away from the person. That is a whole different story.


Kim Soleum opened his eyes. To be met with nothing. In front of his eyes - full absence of light sent him into panic. Did a guard catch up to him? Were his eyes taken? Was the exit a trap? 

The gut feeling, the fundamental instinct told him he was back in the realm of the living, but he craved the relief of a proof. As he tried to move, he felt held. Held tight, with no escape. Something kept him in one place. 

Lying on his back, held in place, now feeling the warmth on his face. 

The paralysing sense of claustrophobic helplessness washed over him. His breathing quickened, mouth desperately holding onto the last sense that grounded him.
 
Where was he? 

Just the thought of a darkness catching him so helplessly, ready to break his ribcage in a swift motion. 

Soleum felt pressure on his face, slowly shifting, tracing the shape of his features. A hand. Exploring, scratching at the edges of his mask. 

“Braun?”




‘He’s shivering.’

The first thoughts Braun had as the darkness left him was the tactile sense of just how fragile the body in his arms was. 

‘He is scared.’

Just out of curiosity, he tightened the grip around his chest, feeling the bones slightly bend under the force. Humans are fragile. You always have to hold yourself to a certain restraint. 

‘He is disoriented.’

Roe Deer’s breathing fastened, his mouth opening for each desperate grasp for air. The temptations, almost taking over, drove Braun to shift his arm to the mask, tracing his fingers over the pitch black matte material of it. 

Just how easy it would be to rip it off: to scratch at the edge, to force it off, to dissect the man piece by piece. How exciting it would be to see what's under, to dive deeper into what’s inside him. To feel the flesh underneath, the living warmth of the human body still breathing the same air as him. 

Humans are fragile…
What is inside a human though? A romantic would say “a soul”. A pragmatic would counter - “flesh and blood”. Braun would argue with both. There was something inside a human that was inaccessible by force. Something so intimate and deeply hidden that you could never pry it open. It held a lock on top of it opening only from one side. It was not soul. Soul was still easy to take if you are a darkness: a trick here, a word there, a nightmare to haunt you to surrender. Contamination did not discriminate: it took equally over the body of flesh and the soul of mind. And yet, contaminated or rotting a body was separate from the real essence he was chasing over. One that could only be willingly surrendered. And thence so much more desirable.

Here, alone, between the two of them, how easy would it be to cast the shadows to completely remove their presence from any prying eyes, to then take what's right within his reach. To push his fingers deeper and deeper, to scratch the man’s heart, to taste his blood and hear his intimate sobs of fear. He would erase the pain. The screams of agony were boring, especially with no audience of poorer taste than his. No. The man in his hands had an innate talent. It was hard to say yet what it was, but every sense in Braun’s body told him there was so much more he could get out of his new friend. See his cry in fear, watch him break in agony, enjoy his short lived happiness and console his inevitable grief. This man could give him a performance of a lifetime. 

No, ending it so quickly would be a waste Braun could never forgive himself for. In his arms was a diamond ready to be polished to perfection. Just for his eyes to shine, for his eyes alone. 

His arm fell from the mask to the man’s chest, feeling the heart beat so fast it made Braun close his eyes to feel it echo through his fingers. 

“Braun?”

His eyes shot wide. 

‘How brave you are, my little Deer.’

“Yes, my friend?”

Braun felt the shake of Soleum’s body, startled at his voice.

‘He still thinks I am contaminated…’

“Have we returned, Mr. Host?”

How clever, Braun admitted. To use just the right words to probe around the situation. 
What a shame this had to be cut short soon. The inevitable memory loss that would follow pushed Braun to lean into an intrusive thought. 

He pushed the body closer, lifting his head to push his face into the soft black hair. Even through the fog of the mask he could admire the soft strands. 

‘How sad it is to know just how much I am about to forget.’

His hair smelt of sweat, dust and just a bit of ash left behind by Braun’s flames. That sent a warm feeling over the Host: being a part of his friend in any way was something so unobtainable and temporary and therefore so thrilling. 

“Yes, we have,” he answered, dragging his face through the hair. “Are you hurt in any way, my friend?”

He could sense the struggle behind every word.

“No, but, could you please let me sit up, Mr. Host?”
In all honesty, no.

“Of course.”

Braun sat up himself, leading the man along, feeling his body tense as they remained close. Finally, he removed his gloved hand from the mask, seeing the man jerk at the bright light. Carefully, the Deer mask turned around, the wide eyes facing his. 

‘What colour are your eyes?’

Roe’s lips thinned into a pout.

‘What does your voice sound not fully hidden from me?’

He gently pushed himself away, getting up from Braun’s lap.

‘How much strength is there in your body?’

He rose up fully, quickly setting the distance between them.

‘Could your fingers leave a bruise on my skin, if you tried hard enough?’




Soleum sprung to his feet, feeling like he was finally able to take a breath: his chest aching from the tight grip. 

Braun, fully relaxed, smiled at him still sitting on the floor: legs crossed, arms laid where Soleum was just held a moment ago. 

First of all: it felt odd to look at him from above. Somehow, the innocent smile and somewhat disheveled hair made him look different from his usual composed demeanor. He felt smaller, a lot less intimidating, while Kim Soleum could still feel the lingering pain of the strength behind his arms.

Second: his face was back. The mask that Soleum could still vividly recall merging into his skin was now gone. Gone was the screen and the TV head, gone was the absurd height and the wires, coat far in the corner of the room shoved away and forgotten. 

The Host was back to being Braun. 

‘This can’t be…’

The more Soleum stared back at Braun, the more alert his senses were. The whole situation was unnervingly bizarre. Just in case, Kim Soleum drove his hand over the mask, checking that it was still attached. Who knows what would have happened had the contaminated Host taken it off him. 

“We are back,” Braun started getting up, straightening his suit. “All thanks to you. And I am sure the rest will follow shortly. Again, thanks to you.”

‘Any time now, please!’

If anything, this was the moment he dreaded when pulling Braun along into the exit. Alone he had no chance to calm down the contaminated man pulled from the darkness. And yet, nothing about Braun gave off any hostility. Or contamination to begin with. 

Was he going insane?

Surely every little part of their encounter in the manor was real. The TV head, the fire, the loud speakers he used. 

‘This is insanity!’

How was he supposed to confirm it all? Directly ask? ‘Hi have you perhaps been contaminated out of your mind just a moment ago?’

But a confirmation came from Braun himself.

“I have to admit though, friend. What you said back there was shocking,” with a dramatic pout he went to pick up the coat that Soleum must have pushed away as they tumbled on the floor. 

When Soleum did not respond, Braun continued, patting dust off the black fabric.

“I hoped you would take better care of the little memento I asked you to keep.”

‘So, he heard.’

In a way, even bringing up the plush was a random improvised idea, he didn’t think the contaminated elite would even register it, and yet. 


“It is safe and sound in my room. All taken care of,” Soleum replied, keeping his eyes directly on the Host, praying someone would come and finally intrude on this unrequested privacy. 

Braun shrugged. 

“A shame. I thought you would keep it around, it would really make me happy to see it sometime!” 

Braun beamed at the thought. Soleum, on the contrary, shivered at the idea. Somehow the desire to get rid of the rabbit as soon as he got home was growing exponentially. 

“I am not su-”

Before he could finish, the doors to the room shot open and in the doorstep Supervisor Park and Chief Lizard immediately turned to him. 

“Roe!”

Park Minseong ran to Soleum and took a close look at him, checking for visible injuries. 

“We were worried when we made the decision to go deeper, thought we lost you or you would not be able to trace us, but then we heard the announcement and-”

“Ah, your squadmates are all safe and sound as you hoped they’d be!”

Supervisor Park straightened, respectfully reaching his arm to Braun. 

“Thank you for taking care of Roe, Mr. Host, it is a relief to know he was in good company!”
Braun accepted the handshake with a honey sweet smile. 

“The pleasure was all mine! After all, that’s one incredible rookie you have here in your squad. Impressive in every aspect,” Braun shot a little wink at Soleum before side-eyeing Lee Jaheon. “Wish you kept better care of such important human resources of your team. Who knows, maybe some elite team will come snatching him from right under your nose.”

The lizard replied straight away:

“The members of the D squad are free to pick where they wish to transfer, but only based on their own choice.”

‘Squad manager Lee, you are my favourite humanoid lizard, thank you.’

Braun squinted, deciding against expressing his already clear frustration. 

“Well, all good that ends well, right, Mr. Roe Deer?” 

Kim Soleum wanted to vanish from right there as soon as the attention was brought back to him.

“It was a pleasure working with you again, Mr. Host,” he murmured quietly. 

Braun waved and headed for the exit. 

“Take care, and remember, Mr. Roe Deer, you owe me a favour, so I hope to see you take good care of our little friend from the park!”

And with that, as the doors behind him closed, Kim Soleum could finally feel alive. 

‘This is MADNESS!’

He was contaminated, he clearly was! And now Soleum was just letting him walk away into the outside world. Was that even possible? He always thought contamination was a less controlled state when first encountered. 

‘But was it his first time…’

So many thoughts raced through his head. 

If it was to be a controlled contamination, could Braun have something to do with the Security team?

“Section Chief,” Soleum decided to try. “Have you ever worked with the Host before?”

“No.”

‘Fair.’

“What about you, Mr. Park?”

Park Minseong hesitated. 

“No way, Roe. Even catching him around is a rare case. Some people around here think he is more of a story than a real employee. Have to admit, the first time I ever saw him was with you after the amusement park incident…”

‘This is madness.’

How was he to prove that the top employee of the company, known for his perfect exploration records of any Darkness, is in fact a darkness himself. With a TV for a head and obsession with talk shows?

After a bunch of reports was submitted to the higher ups, an invitation to participate in contamination checks was issued to all key participants of the events in the manor. 

From what Kim Soleum later heard, no one was tested positive. 

‘How?!’
