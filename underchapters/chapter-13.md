Straightening up, Braun laughed. His face was so close, Soleum could feel a tickle of the warm breath of his cheek. The idea of feeling a presence of another being, on his own corporeal body, so close he could touch the man before him… Deprived of it all, to Soleum it felt like the last salvation was offering itself into his grasp. 

It took Braun all of his restraint not to jolt at the touch. His human’s slim fingers glided over the skin of his face, tracing over the features the Host carefully picked himself. The cheekbones, the nose bridge, a light tap at the brows and a quick scratch of nails over the sensitive skin of his scalp. Involuntary, he leaned further. A good Host returns a remark with a comment, an answer with a question and a touch with a press of his own lips onto the warm skin pulsating in a ragged heartbeat. One sharp inhale, just to remember more. More. There was so much more to take. 

He heard the voice. He saw the face. He touched his skin, he breathed his scent. There was still more he needed before he could understand it all. Before this illusive idea of “humanity” was dissected into pieces for him to assemble blind. 

Hunger. 
For the first time ever he felt raw, unadulterated hunger spark through his veins like electric currents going through his cables. 

“Not like this,” Kim Soleum’s voice was quiet, shaken with terror of his own actions. 

Pulling away, Braun felt the grip of the hands on his face tighten. 

“I want to see the real you.”

There it was, the unspoken hanging between them.

“Can you show me the you that once looked over this place?” 

Gently, Braun took Soleum’s hands off his face and gentle cooling black envelopped his face. From within the dark, his voice echoed in the now so familiar speaker-like tone. 

“By doing this I am cutting the time I have with you, but how can this Braun deny his Friend's request?” 

Driven by the forces beyond his own understanding, Kim Soleum’s arms dug deep into the cold wet shadow, feeling it wrap around his fingers in a tight embrace. 

For a moment there, it was as if both of them could not let go.
