Trying not to dwell much on the reason he was there, Noah rushed through the exit and took a deep breath of the fresh air, feeling a little more alive. 

He had to get out quickly. Unknowing whether or not Anubis could track him down, the best guess was to get away from the places tied to him: especially places where he had died.
