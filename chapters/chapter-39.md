“It’s wonderful, isn’t it?”

Anubis shook his hands off his head and turned to look up at the blind man, grimacing at the stench of bodily fluids in the room. 

“Nothing about this is natural, Love.”

Cupid lowered himself to the same eye level, sitting down by his side and tracing his hand over a side of Cecil’s thigh where the skin was still slightly wet after growing. 

“Don’t you see it?” He could feel where Henry’s touches likely lingered in the past days, imagining a thing or two and knowing he was not far from the truth. The anticipation made him shiver and he turned to Anubis. “This is the solution.”

Even in the dark, even then Death could feel the obsessive look of Love on him. He backed away, but a hand, still damp with sweat and intracellular liquid, clasped his chin and turned his face, forcing him to look at the way the body on the bed twitched lightly when Cupid’s other hand traced over its more exposed parts. 

“This is all you’ve ever wanted,” Cupid whispered, eager to show him the solution to all his problems. How could Death not see, how could Death be more blind than Love itself?!

“I want none of this,” Anubis hissed, trying to get away from the hold, only to have Cupid press down on him with his body weight, nearly sending them tumbling down onto the dirty floor. 

Looking down on him, Cupid smiled. 

"*Love* that follows even through *Death*,” Cupid spoke like a man eager to share a revelation. “*Love* that becomes one with *Death*!”

Cupid grabbed Anubis by the hair and pulled their faces so close together Anubis could feel the warmth of his breath on his skin. 

“ Your true companion! *Love* that outlives *Time*!”
